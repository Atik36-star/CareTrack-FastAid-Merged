Privacy Policy

Emergency Assistance stores account details, emergency descriptions, and any location a user chooses to share so it can support requests and matching. A request's location and description can be shown to nearby approved volunteers; case details are limited by account role and case participation. The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives the map area requested by the browser. This installation does not provide encryption at rest, a deletion-request workflow, or a configured contact channel. Do not submit information you do not want stored in the configured database.
